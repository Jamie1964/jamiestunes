About Atticus Blue

Atticus Blue is an accomplished acoustic busker and singer-songwriter known for bringing unforgettable musical moments to the streets and public squares of London. Armed with an acoustic guitar and a rich vocal style, he frequently collaborates with fellow independent street vocalists like Leire to create captivating spontaneous performances.

Capturing the hearts of online audiences through viral YouTube performances, Atticus Blue covers timeless classics and contemporary hits alike. His engaging public sessions draw large crowds of onlookers, showcasing an authentic connection with live listeners and establishing him as a standout presence in modern street music culture.
