About Andrew Duncan

Andrew Duncan is a talented acoustic street performer and musician known for captivating audiences across iconic London locations like Trafalgar Square and Leicester Square. With soulful vocals and masterful guitar arrangements, his live renditions turn bustling public spaces into intimate concert venues.

Delivering unforgettable covers and heartfelt performances, Andrew Duncan continues to captivate both passing crowds on the street and a growing international audience online.
