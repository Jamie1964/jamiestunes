About Luke Silva

Luke Silva is an internationally acclaimed singer, content creator, and viral street musician who has captured the hearts of millions across the globe. Known for his soulful vocal delivery, emotional depth, and incredible ability to engage with live crowds on street corners and urban plazas, Luke turns ordinary public spaces into breathtaking musical stages.

Rising to massive popularity through viral videos on platforms like TikTok and YouTube—where his channel has amassed over 2 million subscribers—Luke frequently invites talented strangers and passersby to share the microphone with him. His signature performances showcase powerful acoustic renditions of chart-topping hits, emotional ballads, and dynamic covers that draw massive, captivated crowds.

With formal performance training from London's ICMP (Music University & Performance School), Luke balances his viral street busking career with original musical releases and international appearances, cementing his status as one of the most exciting modern vocal talents performing live today.
