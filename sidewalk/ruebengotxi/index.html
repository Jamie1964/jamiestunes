About Rueben & Gotxi

Rueben & Gotxi are a dynamic musical duo renowned for their captivating acoustic street performances and vibrant energy. Bringing crowds together in public squares and walkways, their powerful instrumentation and harmonic vocals create unforgettable open-air musical experiences.

Showcasing their talent across viral platforms, Rueben & Gotxi continue to win over online and offline audiences alike with heartfelt renditions and passionate live sessions that celebrate the true spirit of street busking.
