About Leire

Leire is an exceptionally talented vocalist and street performer known for captivating crowds with emotional, pitch-perfect renditions across London's vibrant streets. Capturing global attention through viral videos, her soulful delivery and remarkable range bring new life to classic anthems and contemporary favorites alike.

Collaborating with fellow musicians and street artists such as Atticus Blue, Leire has built a massive international audience online through platforms like YouTube and TikTok. Her powerful performances showcase deep musicality and an authentic connection with live audiences, cementing her status as one of today's most exciting independent vocal talents.
