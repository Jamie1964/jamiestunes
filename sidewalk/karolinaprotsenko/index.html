About Karolina Protsenko

Karolina Protsenko (born October 3, 2008) is a sensational young violinist and musical prodigy who has captivated millions of listeners worldwide through her breathtaking public street performances and acoustic cover arrangements. Born in Ukraine, Karolina moved with her family to the United States (settling in California) when she was young, and she began playing the violin at the age of six.

Rising to international prominence through her viral online videos and dedicated digital community, she performs a diverse repertoire spanning popular contemporary hits, timeless classics, rock anthems, and cinematic themes. Her joyful stage presence, expressive technique, and ability to connect deeply with outdoor crowds have transformed public spaces into vibrant concert venues, inspiring aspiring musicians globally.
