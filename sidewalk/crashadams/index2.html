About Crash Adams

Crash Adams is a vibrant Canadian pop-rock duo hailing from Toronto, consisting of childhood best friends Rafaele "Crash" Massarelli and Vince "Adams" Sasso. Known for their infectious, genre-defying sound that blends modern pop hooks with classic rock energy, the duo has captured global attention through both their catchy studio releases and unique viral marketing stunts.

Rising to international prominence by hauling a bright red couch into unexpected public spaces for impromptu street performances and TikTok videos, Crash Adams has built an enormous worldwide following. Signed to Warner Music Canada, they have released fan-favorite EPs and albums including California Girl and their Crashing Into Your Living Room volumes, delivering chart-climbing hits like "Give Me A Kiss" and "New Heart."

With multi-instrumental talents, dynamic songwriting, and an energetic performance style reminiscent of pop icons, Crash Adams continues to redefine how artists connect directly with fans on the streets and online.
