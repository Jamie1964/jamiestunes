Concert Details

Artist: Rod Stewart

Concert Name: Rod Stewart - AVO Session Basel Full Concert

Year: 2012

Venue: Event Halle, Messe Basel (Switzerland)

Event Series: AVO Session Basel (now known as Baloise Session)

Background & Performance Notes

Recorded live during the prestigious AVO Session in Basel, Switzerland, this intimate 2012 concert showcases Rod Stewart in peak form. Backed by his phenomenal touring band and backing vocalists, Stewart balances raspy rock energy with stripped-down acoustic arrangements, treating the audience to a masterclass in live vocal performance and stage charisma.

Setlist & Musical Highlights

The performance features a stellar mix of his timeless solo catalog and classic covers. Known for the festival's intimate club-style table seating, the AVO Session allows Stewart to interact closely with the crowd, delivering soulful renditions of iconic anthems like "Maggie May," "Forever Young," and his signature raspy acoustic ballads.

Fun Facts & Trivia

  • The AVO Session Basel is celebrated for bringing legendary international artists into an intimate, club-like setting where fans sit at candlelit tables close to the stage.
  • Rod Stewart's distinctive raspy voice stems from a benign nodule removed from his vocal cords early in his career, as well as his natural gravelly tone.
  • He has sold over 250 million records worldwide, making him one of the best-selling music artists in history.
