Concert Details

Artist: Journey

Concert Name: Journey - Live in Houston (November 6th, 1981)

Year: 1981

Venue: The Summit, Houston, Texas

Tour: Escape Tour

Background & Performance Notes

Captured at the height of their commercial dominance during the blockbuster Escape tour, this legendary concert at The Summit in Houston showcases Journey firing on all cylinders. Featuring Steve Perry's soaring, unmistakable vocals and Neal Schon's blistering guitar work, this performance stands as one of the definitive live documents of 1980s arena rock.

Setlist & Musical Highlights

The setlist is a masterclass in melodic rock, blending career-defining anthems from the *Escape* album—such as "Don't Stop Believin'", "Who's Crying Now", and "Open Arms"—with explosive fan favorites from earlier records like *Departure* and *Infinity*.

Fun Facts & Trivia

  • The *Escape* album hit number one on the Billboard 200 and ultimately spawned multiple global smash hit singles.
  • The Houston concert was professionally filmed and broadcast, becoming heavily bootlegged before receiving official high-definition restorations.
  • Steve Perry's vocal prowess during this era cemented his reputation as one of rock music's greatest and most influential frontmen.
