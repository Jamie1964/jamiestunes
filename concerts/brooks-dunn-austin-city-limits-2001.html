Concert Details

Artist: Brooks & Dunn

Concert Name: Austin City Limits

Date: 2001

Venue: ACL Studio, Austin, TX

Event Type: Live Television Concert Performance

Background & Performance Notes

Recorded during their absolute prime in 2001, country music's most successful duo Kix Brooks and Ronnie Dunn took to the legendary *Austin City Limits* stage for an intimate yet electrifying live performance. Known for blending honky-tonk swagger with soaring vocal harmonies and driving rock-infused rhythms, the duo delivered a masterclass in modern country showmanship before an adoring studio audience.

Setlist & Musical Highlights

This stellar setlist features career-defining anthems and emotional ballads, including foot-stomping hits like "Brand New Man," "Boot Scootin' Boogie," "Neon Moon," "Only in America," and "My Maria." Ronnie Dunn's towering soulful vocals paired with Kix Brooks' high-energy stage presence showcase why they reigned as country radio titans for over two decades.

Fun Facts & Legacy Trivia

  • *Austin City Limits* is the longest-running popular music series in American television history, celebrating the finest roots and country artists.
  • Brooks & Dunn earned dozens of CMA and ACM Awards, cementing their legacy as one of the definitive acts in country music history.
  • Their 2001 appearance coincided with the release of their hit album *Steers & Stripes*, further expanding their massive chart dominance.
