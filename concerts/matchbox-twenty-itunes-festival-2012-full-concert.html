Concert Details

Artist: Matchbox Twenty

Concert Name: iTunes Festival 2012

Date: September 7, 2012

Venue: The Roundhouse, London, United Kingdom

Tour: North Tour / iTunes Festival Residency

Background & Production Notes

Performed live at London's historic The Roundhouse during the month-long 2012 iTunes Festival, this concert captured Matchbox Twenty in peak form. The appearance served as a high-profile showcase right around the release of their long-awaited fourth studio album, North, which marked their first full-length collection of new material in ten years.

Frontman Rob Thomas and the band delivered an electrifying set that seamlessly blended fresh tracks from North with iconic multi-platinum hits from their rich catalog, establishing an intimate yet arena-sized connection with the London audience.

Setlist Highlights & Performance Style

The performance balances infectious upbeat anthems like "Bent", "Push", and "3AM" with soaring modern additions such as "She's So Mean" and "Overjoyed". Rob Thomas's charismatic stage presence combined with tight vocal harmonies and driving guitar riffs keeps the energy high throughout the entire hour-long set.

Fun Facts & Trivia

  • North debuted at No. 1 on the US Billboard 200 chart, making it the band's first number-one album in their career.
  • The iTunes Festival (later renamed Apple Music Festival) took place annually in London's Camden Roundhouse, featuring iconic intimate gigs by world-renowned artists.
  • Rob Thomas is widely recognized as one of modern rock's premier songwriters, having also penned the smash hit "Smooth" with Santana.
  • The band played a fast-paced, hit-packed 15-song set specifically curated for the festival broadcast.

Spotlight

Matchbox Twenty — Rob Thomas, Paul Doucette, Brian Yale, and Kyle Cook. Masters of hook-laden post-grunge and alternative pop-rock melodies that defined late '90s and 2000s radio.
