Background & Performance Notes

Bringing their signature blend of rock, jazz fusion, and glorious horn arrangements to Kansas, the legendary band Chicago delivers a masterclass in live musicianship on their 2024 tour. Performing before an enthusiastic crowd in the Wichita area, the group showcases their timeless catalog with immaculate vocal harmonies, blistering brass sections, and high-energy stage presence.

Setlist & Musical Highlights

This stellar performance features triumphant renditions of their most beloved career-spanning hits, including energetic takes on "Saturday in the Park," "25 or 6 to 4," "You're the Inspiration," "Hard to Say I'm Sorry," and "Make Me Smile."

Fun Facts & Legacy Trivia

  • Formed in Chicago in 1967, the band is one of the longest-running and best-selling rock groups of all time, boasting record sales exceeding 100 million.
  • Chicago was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2016, cementing their profound influence on American pop and rock radio.
  • Their distinctive horn section—originally featuring trombone, trumpet, and saxophone—became the blueprint for horn-rock bands across the globe.
