Concert Details

Artist: Fleetwood Mac

Concert Name: The Dance

Date: May 1997 (Released August 1997)

Venue: Warner Bros. Studios, Burbank, California

Tour: The Dance Reunion Tour

Background & Production Notes

Recorded live on a soundstage at Warner Bros. Studios in Burbank, California, The Dance marked the triumphant and highly anticipated return of Fleetwood Mac's classic mid-1970s lineup: Mick Fleetwood, John McVie, Christine McVie, Lindsey Buckingham, and Stevie Nicks. The concert commemorated the 20th anniversary of their blockbuster 1977 album, Rumours.

The intimate yet grand scale of the production allowed for powerful acoustic arrangements alongside roaring stadium-level renditions of their greatest hits, capturing the unmistakable chemistry and enduring emotional depth of the band members.

Setlist Highlights & Performance Style

The concert features breathtaking performances of timeless classics including "Dreams", "Go Your Own Way", "Gypsy", "Landslide" (featuring a deeply moving acoustic guitar and vocal delivery), and "The Chain". Augmented by a full backing orchestra and choir on select tracks, the setlist serves as a definitive masterclass in classic rock songwriting and vocal harmony.

Fun Facts & Trivia

  • The Dance debuted at No. 1 on the US Billboard 200 chart and became one of the best-selling live albums of all time.
  • It was the first time the classic five-piece lineup had performed and recorded together since the 1982 Mirage tour.
  • The performance of "Landslide" from this concert was released as a single and became a major radio hit all over again, introducing the band to a brand new generation of fans.
  • President Bill Clinton famously used Fleetwood Mac's "Don't Stop" as the theme song for his 1992 presidential campaign, leading to this iconic lineup reuniting at his inaugural ball in 1993, which paved the way for this full reunion tour.

Spotlight

Fleetwood Mac — Mick Fleetwood, Stevie Nicks, Lindsey Buckingham, Christine McVie, and John McVie. Legendary architects of confessional pop-rock, immortal vocal harmonies, and timeless sonic tapestries.
