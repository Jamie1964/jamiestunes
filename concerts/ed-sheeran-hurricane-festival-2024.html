Concert Details

Artist: Ed Sheeran

Concert Name: Live at Hurricane Festival (Full Set)

Date: June 2024

Venue: Eichenring, Scheeßel, Germany

Event Type: Major European Music Festival Headline Set

Background & Performance Notes

Headlining the prestigious Hurricane Festival in Germany, global superstar Ed Sheeran delivered a masterclass in solo live performance. Armed with just his acoustic guitar, custom loop pedal, and boundless energy, Sheeran commanded a massive sea of festivalgoers. Without a backing band, he builds every layered rhythm, vocal harmony, and driving percussion track live on stage, demonstrating the peerless musicianship and crowd control that turned him into one of the biggest touring acts in music history.

Setlist & Musical Highlights

This powerhouse festival setlist spans his record-breaking catalog, seamlessly blending heartfelt acoustic ballads with explosive stadium anthems. Fans are treated to soaring sing-alongs of massive hits like "Bad Habits," "Shape of You," "Thinking Out Loud," "Castle on the Hill," and "Perfect." His ability to transform a sprawling German festival field into an intimate campfire sing-along highlights his rare gift for live connection.

Fun Facts & Festival Trivia

  • The Hurricane Festival is one of Germany's premier annual rock and alternative music festivals, drawing tens of thousands of passionate music fans to the Eichenring racetrack in Scheeßel.
  • Ed Sheeran's signature loop station setup, affectionately named "Chewie," has been his primary live companion across thousands of shows worldwide.
  • His 2024 festival appearances formed part of his massive worldwide touring schedule, breaking attendance records across multiple continents.
