Concert Details

Artist: Dolly Parton

Concert Name: Austin City Limits Live

Date: 2022

Venue: ACL Live at The Moody Theater, Austin, TX

Event Type: Special Live Performance Showcase

Background & Performance Notes

Featuring her trademark warmth, sparkling humor, and peerless vocal prowess, global icon Dolly Parton takes center stage for this breathtaking live showcase. Performing before an ecstatic audience in Austin, Dolly effortlessly bridges generations of music lovers, delivering stunning acoustic arrangements, heartfelt storytelling, and unforgettable melodies that highlight her status as one of the greatest songwriters and entertainers of all time.

Setlist & Musical Highlights

This stellar performance features breathtaking renditions of her most beloved chart-topping classics, including transcendent takes on "Jolene," "9 to 5," "I Will Always Love You," "Coat of Many Colors," and "Here You Come Again." Her intimate banter and dazzling stage presence make every song feel deeply personal.

Fun Facts & Legacy Trivia

  • Dolly Parton has composed over 3,000 songs across her legendary career, earning induction into both the Country Music Hall of Fame and the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame.
  • *Austin City Limits* has hosted music legends for over five decades, serving as the definitive television stage for American roots and country music excellence.
  • Her timeless songwriting continues to inspire artists across all genres, from pop and rock to bluegrass and contemporary country.
