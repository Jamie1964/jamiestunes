Concert Details

Artist: Chicago

Concert Name: Live at Tanglewood

Date: 1970

Venue: Tanglewood Music Center, Lenox, MA

Event Type: Vintage Live Concert Broadcast

Background & Performance Notes

Captured during their blazing formative years in 1970, this legendary performance at the historic Tanglewood venue showcases Chicago operating as an absolute powerhouse of experimental rock and jazz-fusion. Long before stadium tours and pop ballad dominance, the original lineup (Terry Kath, Robert Lamm, Peter Cetera, Danny Seraphine, Lee Loughnane, James Pankow, and Walter Parazaider) delivered raw, uninhibited musical wizardry that left audiences spellbound.

Setlist & Musical Highlights

This phenomenal setlist dives deep into their first two landmark albums (*Chicago Transit Authority* and *Chicago II*), featuring extended psychedelic jams, blistering horn arrangements, and passionate vocals on masterpieces like "25 or 6 to 4," "Beginnings," "Does Anybody Really Know What Time It Is?," and "Ballet for a Girl in Buchannon."

Fun Facts & Legacy Trivia

  • Guitar legend Jimi Hendrix was famously an enormous fan of Chicago during this era, frequently praising Terry Kath's unmatched guitar prowess and vocal style.
  • Tanglewood is traditionally the summer home of the Boston Symphony Orchestra, making rock acts performing on its stage in 1970 a groundbreaking cultural crossover.
  • This vintage concert recording stands as a holy grail for fans of early-70s progressive rock and brass-infused improvisational music.
