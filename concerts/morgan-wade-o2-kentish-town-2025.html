Artist: Morgan Wade

Concert Name: Full Concert Live at O2 Forum Kentish Town

Background & Performance Notes

On March 25, 2025, celebrated country-rock singer-songwriter Morgan Wade took the stage at London's historic O2 Forum Kentish Town for an arresting, stripped-back performance. Known for her raw, unfiltered lyricism, distinctive raspy vocals, and deep emotional honesty, Wade delivered a powerful set that highlighted her exceptional storytelling ability. Stripping away heavy production to focus purely on guitars, keyboards, and acoustic resonance, the evening created an unforgettable, up-close connection with her UK fanbase.

Setlist & Musical Highlights

This remarkable live set draws heavily from her critically acclaimed releases, featuring soul-baring tracks like "Phantom Feeling," "Matches and Metaphors," "East Coast," "Take Me Away," and "Psychopath." Her gritty yet vulnerable vocal delivery shines brightest during the quieter, introspective moments, bridging traditional country twang with alternative rock grit and captivating the London audience from start to finish.

Fun Facts & Venue Trivia

The O2 Forum Kentish Town is widely celebrated for its stunning art deco interior and intimate acoustics, making it a legendary London landmark for stripped-down artist showcases. Morgan Wade's performances across the UK have played a massive role in sparking a booming appreciation for authentic American roots and modern outlaw country music overseas.
