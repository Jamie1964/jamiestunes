About the Performance

Recorded live in an intimate studio setting for Magic Radio, this exclusive showcase captures Ed Sheeran at his absolute best. Relying solely on his acoustic guitar, vocal delivery, and signature loop station setup, Ed demonstrates the raw talent that turned him into one of the defining singer-songwriters of his generation. From nostalgic anthems like "Castle on the Hill" and emotional ballads like "Photograph" and "Perfect" to rhythm-driven tracks like "Shape of You", this session gives fans a front-row seat to an unforgettable musical experience.

Whether you are reliving his classic era or appreciating the intricate timing required to layer live vocal harmonies and guitar loops completely solo, this Magic Radio session remains a fan-favorite archive piece within The Concert Room collection.
