Concert Details

Artist: Elvis Presley

Concert Name: CBS Television Special (Elvis in Concert)

Original Air Date: October 3, 1977

Recorded: June 19 (Omaha, NE) & June 21, 1977 (Rapid City, SD)

Event Type: Posthumous Network Television Concert Special

Background & Performance Notes

Originally filmed by CBS during Elvis Presley's final summer 1977 tour across Omaha, Nebraska, and Rapid City, South Dakota, this historic television special serves as a poignant visual document of the King of Rock 'n' Roll's final stage performances. Though broadcast posthumously in October 1977 just weeks after his passing, the concert footage captures the immense warmth, powerful vocal command, and deep emotional rapport Elvis shared with his devoted fans right up to the end of his legendary career.

Setlist & Musical Highlights

The special features incredible live renditions of timeless classics and powerful late-career emotional highlights. Memorable performances include soaring vocal showcases on "Unchained Melody," "How Great Thou Art," "Are You Lonesome Tonight?", "You Gave Me a Mountain," and rock-and-roll standards like "See See Rider," "Jailhouse Rock," and "Hound Dog," backed by his longtime TCB Band and Joe Guercio's orchestra.

Fun Facts & Legacy Trivia

  • The CBS television broadcast drew massive ratings upon its initial airing in October 1977, becoming one of the most-watched network music specials of the era.
  • The accompanying live soundtrack album released simultaneously by RCA Records achieved massive commercial success, eventually reaching multi-platinum status.
  • This special remains one of the only officially filmed and recorded multi-camera concert productions capturing Elvis during his final touring years.
