George Strait – Live at Allegiant Stadium (2021)

Experience the King of Country music live from Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas. This monumental stadium show features an incredible setlist of timeless hits, grand stage production, and an unforgettable celebration of traditional country music.

Concert Overview & Production Notes

Artist: George Strait

Venue: Allegiant Stadium, Las Vegas, Nevada

Event Date: November 13, 2021

Historical Context: Marking one of his monumental stadium appearances following the return of live entertainment, George Strait delivered a masterclass in traditional country music history at Las Vegas's state-of-the-art Allegiant Stadium. Supported by elite special guests and his legendary backing band, the King of Country treated the massive sold-out crowd to decades of chart-topping anthems with pristine audio engineering and massive stadium-scale visual production.

Featured Setlist Classics

  • Here for a Good Time
  • Check Yes or No
  • Ocean Front Property
  • The Chair
  • Amarillo by Morning
  • All My Ex's Live in Texas
  • The Cowboy Rides Away

Band Member Spotlight

George Strait: Lead vocals and acoustic guitar, anchoring the performance with his signature timeless delivery.

The Ace in the Hole Band: The legendary backing ensemble driving the authentic twin-fiddle, steel-guitar, and honky-tonk rhythm section.
