Concert Details

Artist: Don Henley

Concert Name: Live Inside Job (2000)

Year: 2000

Event Type: Solo World Tour / Concert Special

Background & Performance Notes

The turn of the millennium marked a major creative and touring resurgence for Don Henley, underscored by the release of his acclaimed solo album Inside Job—his first studio record in over a decade. The supporting world tour showcased Henley's trademark perfectionism, deep lyrical depth, and powerhouse vocal delivery. Backed by an incredible touring ensemble of world-class instrumentalists and backing vocalists, Henley bridged his iconic 1980s solo catalog with nuanced, reflective new millennial material.

Setlist & Musical Highlights

This comprehensive concert performance captures the full scope of Henley's legendary musicianship. Fan favorites like "Dirty Laundry," "Sunset Grill," "The Boys of Summer," and "The End of the Innocence" sit seamlessly alongside fresh standout tracks from the Inside Job record such as "Workin' It" and "Taking You Home." The concert also weaves in unforgettable acoustic and emotional highlights including "The Heart of the Matter" and timeless acoustic arrangements.

Fun Facts & Trivia

  • Inside Job was Don Henley's first solo studio release since 1989's multi-platinum masterpiece The End of the Innocence.
  • The tour featured elaborate staging, pristine audio production, and deep visual storytelling that mirrored Henley's socially conscious songwriting themes.
  • Henley balanced his solo tour commitments simultaneously with high-profile reunion events and stadium runs with the Eagles.
