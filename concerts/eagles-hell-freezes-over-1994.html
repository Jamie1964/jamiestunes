Concert Details

Artist: Eagles

Concert Name: Eagles - Hell Freezes Over (1994)

Year: 1994

Venue: Warner Bros. Studios, Burbank, California

Event Type: MTV Special / Live Reunion Concert

Background & Performance Notes

Recorded in April 1994 for an MTV special, Hell Freezes Over marked the triumphant and long-awaited reunion of the Eagles 14 years after their bitter 1980 breakup. True to Glenn Frey's famous quip that the band would only reunite when "hell freezes over," the group stepped onto the soundstage in Burbank to deliver a masterclass in harmony-driven rock. The performance seamlessly blends stripped-down acoustic arrangements with powerhouse electric anthems, proving that their timeless songwriting and signature vocal blends had only deepened with time.

Setlist & Musical Highlights

The concert famously opens with Don Henley telling the audience, "For the record, we never broke up; we just took a 14-year vacation." It features groundbreaking acoustic reworkings of classic tracks—most notably a mesmerizing, Spanish-guitar intro version of "Hotel California"—alongside fresh studio tracks written for the companion live album, including "Get Over It" and "Love Will Keep Us Alive."

Fun Facts & Trivia

  • The accompanying live album debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 and went on to sell millions of copies worldwide, spawning a massive global tour.
  • The acoustic arrangement of "Hotel California" became one of the most celebrated live acoustic television broadcasts of the 1990s.
  • The lineup featured core members Glenn Frey, Don Henley, Joe Walsh, Don Felder, and Timothy B. Schmit.
