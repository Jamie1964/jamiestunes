Background & Performance Notes

Renowned for his distinctively bluesy baritone vocals, long hair, and high-octane blend of traditional country and Southern rock attitude, Travis Tritt commands the stage in this explosive *Live & Kickin'* concert special. As one of the leading figures of country music's monumental early-1990s boom, Tritt brings unmatched energy, stellar guitar work, and raw emotional depth to every song, treating fans to a masterclass in modern outlaw performance.

Setlist & Musical Highlights

This powerhouse setlist packs a serious punch, featuring career-defining smash hits and crowd favorites like "T-R-O-U-B-L-E," "It's a Great Day to Be Alive," "Anymore," "Modern Day Bonnie and Clyde," "Foolish Pride," and "Country Club." His fiery guitar solos and soul-stirring vocal delivery keep the audience completely captivated from the first chord.

Fun Facts & Legacy Trivia

  • Travis Tritt is a multi-Grammy Award winner and Grand Ole Opry member, celebrated for helping pioneer the genre-bending 'hat acts' era of country music.
  • His gritty, blues-influenced vocal style was heavily inspired by classic Southern rock legends like the Allman Brothers Band and Waylon Jennings.
  • Concert specials like *Live & Kickin'* capture the raw electricity of his touring band, cementing his reputation as one of country music's most dynamic live entertainers.
