Concert Details

Artist: Shania Twain

Concert Name: Legendary Concert in Chicago (Up! Live)

Date: July 27, 2003

Venue: Hutchinson Field, Grant Park, Chicago, IL

Event Type: Open-Air Live Concert Special

Background & Performance Notes

Filmed live on July 27, 2003, at Hutchinson Field in Chicago's Grant Park, this monumental open-air concert captured the global Queen of Country Pop at the absolute peak of her superstardom. Performing before a roaring crowd of over 50,000 ecstatic fans, Shania Twain delivered an electrifying, high-energy spectacle supporting her blockbuster diamond-certified album *Up!*. Directed by Beth McCarthy-Miller, the television special drew massive audiences worldwide, cementing itself as one of the definitive concert films of the decade.

Setlist & Musical Highlights

This legendary career-spanning setlist overflows with high-octane crossover anthems and deeply emotional sing-alongs. From the show-stopping opener "Man! I Feel Like A Woman!" and the infectious title track "Up!" to crowd favorites like "That Don't Impress Me Much," "From This Moment On," and "You're Still The One," Shania's powerhouse vocals and magnetic stage presence command the vast Chicago parkland from the first note to the explosive final encore of "Rock This Country!"

Fun Facts & Venue Trivia

  • The Chicago concert special originally premiered on NBC in August 2003, pulling in an incredible 8.87 million viewers nationwide.
  • Shania's iconic bright yellow hooded outfit from this performance has become instantly recognizable to fans worldwide as a defining visual symbol of the *Up!* era.
  • Grant Park's vast open-air layout allowed for massive stage production, immersive lighting, and dynamic camera angles that captured the sheer scale of her touring peak.
