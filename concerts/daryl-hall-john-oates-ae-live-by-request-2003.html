Concert Details

Artist: Daryl Hall & John Oates

Concert Name: A&E Live by Request

Date: 2003

Venue: A&E Studios, New York, NY

Event Type: Interactive Live Television Concert Special

Background & Performance Notes

Recorded for A&E's acclaimed *Live by Request* series in 2003, this special showcases pop-rock history's most successful duo, Daryl Hall and John Oates, performing in an intimate setting driven entirely by live viewer phone calls and emails. Known for their silky harmonies, infectious blue-eyed soul grooves, and impeccable songwriting, Hall and Oates share engaging banter, take spontaneous requests, and deliver pristine acoustic and full-band renditions of their multi-platinum catalog.

Setlist & Musical Highlights

This dynamic setlist spans decades of chart dominance, featuring smooth and soulful renditions of iconic radio staples like "Rich Girl," "Private Eyes," "Maneater," "I Can't Go for That (No Can Do)," "Sara Smile," and "Out of Touch." The unscripted nature of the broadcast allows for a relaxed, joyful atmosphere that highlights their enduring vocal chemistry and tight touring band.

Fun Facts & Legacy Trivia

  • A&E's *Live by Request* was famous for putting artists on the spot without a fixed setlist, relying entirely on real-time viewer interaction from fans around the world.
  • Daryl Hall and John Oates remain the best-selling duo in music history, scoring numerous Billboard Hot 100 number-one hits throughout the 1970s and 1980s.
  • This 2003 television special captures the duo during a major resurgence in appreciation for their classic songwriting and catalog influence among modern artists.
