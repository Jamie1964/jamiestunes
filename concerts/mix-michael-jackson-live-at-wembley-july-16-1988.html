Background & Production Notes

Recorded live on July 16, 1988, at London's iconic Wembley Stadium, this performance stands as one of the definitive live documents of the King of Pop. Part of the record-breaking Bad World Tour, the concert was attended by 72,000 fans, including Prince Charles and Princess Diana.

The setlist features groundbreaking renditions of chart-topping hits from the Bad and Thriller albums, including "Wanna Be Startin' Somethin'", "Smooth Criminal", "Beat It", "Billie Jean", and "Man in the Mirror". Renowned for its flawless choreography, powerful live vocals, and unmatched stage production, the Wembley concert remains a masterclass in pop performance history.

Setlist Highlights & Performance Style

Michael Jackson's stage presence during the Bad tour combined high-energy athletic choreography with precise vocal control. The performance captured the absolute peak of his solo touring career, featuring iconic costume changes, complex lighting designs, and legendary live band arrangements.

Fun Facts & Trivia

  • Attended by 72,000 ecstatic fans on opening night at Wembley, welcoming British royalty Prince Charles and Princess Diana.
  • The Bad World Tour was Michael Jackson's first solo concert tour, grossing over $125 million globally.
  • This specific July 16 show is widely considered by music historians and fans to be among the finest captured live recordings of his entire career.
  • Features the legendary debut live performances of several Billboard Hot 100 number-one hit singles.
