Background & Production Notes

A Guitar Night showcases the extraordinary talents of 40 Fingers, an internationally acclaimed acoustic guitar quartet consisting of Matteo Brenci, Emanuele Grafitti, Enrico Maria Milanesi, and Andrea Lombardini. Utilizing four acoustic guitars, the ensemble weaves intricate fingerstyle arrangements that blend rhythm, percussion, basslines, and soaring melodic leads into a unified wall of sound.

The full concert highlights their unique ability to transform well-known musical standards across genres into mesmerizing acoustic masterpieces. Their seamless interplay and virtuosic precision create an immersive sonic experience that captivates guitar enthusiasts and casual music lovers alike.

Repertoire & Performance Style

The performance features brilliant acoustic interpretations of classic rock anthems, cinematic movie themes, and contemporary pop hits. By treating their instruments simultaneously as percussion units and harmonic boards, the quartet achieves a rich orchestral texture without relying on backing tracks or electronic looping.

Fun Facts & Trivia

  • The ensemble's name represents the total number of fingers playing across the four guitar fretboards.
  • Their viral acoustic cover of Queen's "Bohemian Rhapsody" amassed millions of views across global digital platforms.
  • Each member brings a distinct musical background, ranging from jazz and blues to classical and rock fingerstyle techniques.
  • 40 Fingers has collaborated with renowned artists and performed on prominent international stages.
