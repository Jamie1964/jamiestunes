About Yoyoka

Yoyoka Soma (born October 2009 in Hokkaido, Japan) is a world-renowned Japanese drummer, musician, and internet sensation. Immersed in music from infancy by her parents who run a family band, she began playing the drums at just two years old and started performing live at age four.

Yoyoka shot to international fame after participating in the Hit Like A Girl contest, followed by viral performances covering legendary rock tracks by bands like Led Zeppelin, Deep Purple, and Heart. Praised by musical icons such as Ian Paice and Robert Plant, she represents the pinnacle of young musical excellence, breaking boundaries across global stages and collaborative projects.
