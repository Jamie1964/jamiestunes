The Graystones

The Graystones are an exceptionally talented group of young musicians celebrated for their remarkable instrumentation, tight vocal arrangements, and captivating interpretations of classic rock and pop anthems. Bringing together raw youthful energy with a deep passion for live performance, the group has built an inspiring musical profile through their dynamic covers and dedication to the art of performance. Whether delivering intricate guitar arrangements or shared vocal harmonies, The Graystones continue to charm audiences and stand out as a uniquely gifted young ensemble.

The Graystones - Live Performance: First Set (Part 1)

Experience the opening set of this incredible live performance. Recorded live on October 11, 2025, the band showcases an amazing lineup of covers including Billy Joel's "This Is My Life", the Eagles' "I Can't Tell You Why", Fleetwood Mac's "Landslide" and "Dreams", Teddy Swims' "The Door", Billy Joel's "Vienna", and Postmodern Jukebox's vintage arrangement of Radiohead's "Creep".

The Graystones - Live Performance: Second Set (Part 2)

Continue watching the live performance with the second set! Recorded on October 11, 2025, this segment features fantastic energetic covers including Billie Eilish's "Birds of a Feather", Chris Stapleton's "Cold", Rihanna's "Love on the Brain", Bruno Mars' "Runaway Baby", INXS's "Never Tear Us Apart", Billy Joel's "Movin' Out (Anthony's Song)", Supertramp's "Take the Long Way Home" and "The Logical Song", Stevie Wonder's "Don't You Worry 'bout a Thing", and Journey's "Don't Stop Believin'".

The Graystones - Live Performance: Encore Third Set (Part 3)

Wrap up the show with the third and final set! Recorded on October 11, 2025, this encore segment features standout performances including Sade's "Smooth Operator", Supertramp's "The Logical Song", a Postmodern Jukebox-style rendition of Radiohead's "Creep", and a final energetic closing performance of Journey's "Don't Stop Believin'".
