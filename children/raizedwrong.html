Artist Profile: Raized Wrong

Members: Landon Goldberg, Lyric Goldberg, and Donny Goldberg

Origin: Southern California, USA

Musical Style: Classic Rock, Pop Harmonies, Vintage Covers Reimagined

Genre: Family Band / Next-Gen Performance

Band Background & Origins

Raized Wrong is an energetic, highly acclaimed Southern California family band taking social media and international platforms by storm. Composed of young brothers Landon and Lyric Goldberg alongside their multitalented father Donny, the group specializes in breathing new life into legendary classic tracks from the 1960s, '70s, '80s, and beyond.

Musical Lineage & Style

Music runs deep in the family: Donny comes from a rich musical background (performing alongside family in a notable Beach Boys tribute project called Surf's Up), passing down his expertise in intricate vocal arrangements and vintage instrumentation directly to his sons. With millions of followers across platforms like TikTok and YouTube, Landon and Lyric have captured audiences worldwide with their tight sibling harmonies, instrumental talent, and infectious stage presence.

Fun Facts & Highlights

  • Represent a true third-generation musical family tradition rooted in classic harmony styling.
  • Known for seamlessly bridging generations by introducing timeless rock anthems to Gen Z audiences.
  • Built a massive global following of over 1.5 million fans through viral cover sessions and energetic performances.
