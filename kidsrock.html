About the Project & Performance

Kids Rock For Kids is an inspiring global initiative uniting exceptionally talented young musicians and youth rock bands from across the globe. Through high-energy performances of rock classics, the project highlights world-class young talent while raising awareness and support for charitable causes.

These standout performances showcase gifted kids and teens delivering jaw-dropping technical proficiency—complete with soaring lead vocals, driving guitar riffs, heavy basslines, and tight, professional-grade drumming.
